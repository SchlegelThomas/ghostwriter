Founder decisions (accepted 2026-08-01)

  1. Landing: both Entity Drafts and an Agent-pane job-bundle strip.
  2. Execution: parallel for cheap/read jobs; sequential for heavy prose (e.g. Scene Partner brief → then Dialogue Coach).
  3. Scene Partner: open Plans with a prefilled brief (capture seeded from the job brief).
  4. Story knowledge: propose-only SK draft; writer Acknowledge creates Cast entry.
  5. Spend: Submit spends BYOK immediately (no per-job confirm). Future product limits are out of scope for this plan.
  6. Entry: structured plan turns and free-form chat (“do all 4” / “submit”).

Intent

When the Agent dock proposes a multi-step plan (catalog agents, Scene Partner brief, story-knowledge capture, cast/continuity checks), the writer can Submit and Ghostwriter actually runs the work — not narrate a fake queue. Results land as ADR 0011/0014 proposals (Entity Drafts) while a dock strip tracks the bundle. Propose ≠ apply for manuscript; SK Acknowledge is the explicit create gate.

Out of scope: autonomous background swarms while away; silent manuscript writes; per-job spend confirm UI; durable server-side worker queue; Playwright until founder verifies the complete outcome.

Acceptance criteria

  • Writer can Submit a work-plan-v1 from an Agent turn (chip or free-form “submit”) and see a dock job strip with per-job status.
  • Catalog jobs (e.g. Dialogue Coach, Continuity Reader) actually call catalog-runs and refresh Entity Drafts.
  • Scene Partner jobs create a Plans capture from the brief and deep-link Scene Partner.
  • Story-knowledge jobs create a ready story-knowledge-create-v1 draft; Acknowledge creates the Cast record.
  • Heavy prose jobs run sequentially after parallel cheap jobs (policy C).
  • Chat can emit a work plan via tool; “submit” without a plan refuses clearly.
  • Refusal when scene/target missing for a job that requires it.

Product model

Job kinds

KindClassExecutionResult
run-catalog-agent cheap (default) Parallel within wave Entity Draft (memo / findings)
create-story-knowledge cheap Parallel SK create draft → Acknowledge → Cast
open-scene-partner heavy Sequential wave after cheap Plans capture + Scene Partner deep-link
cast-reference-check cheap Parallel → Continuity Reader or Character Coach · Cast when id known Entity Draft

Waves

  1. Wave A — all cheap jobs in parallel.
  2. Wave B — heavy jobs one-at-a-time (Scene Partner, then any sequential catalog the plan marks heavy, e.g. Dialogue Coach after partner when both present).

Dialogue Coach alone is cheap/parallel. When the same plan also contains open-scene-partner, Dialogue Coach is deferred to wave B after partner opens (policy C).
