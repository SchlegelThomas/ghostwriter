Ghostwriter delivery plan

Cast & relationships studio

Status: proposed
Branch: feat/capture-to-story-agents (shared shell/UI)
Owner: founder + parent agent
Related: Cursor-shell navigation, Structure / cast browse

Intent

Make Cast a first-class center workspace: Characters rail keeps the manuscript Explorer in the primary side and opens a richer Cast studio in the editor center (same hosting class as Title Page / Plans). Writers should see a character’s role in the story, a readable relationship map, and scene presence — not a cramped sidebar form that replaces the tree.

Out of scope: force-directed physics graph libraries; AI-authored role summaries; new canonical relationship kinds; Canvas board rewrite; Playwright until founder verifies.

Acceptance criteria

  • Characters rail opens Cast in the center; primary side remains Explorer (tree stays visible).
  • Cast roster (all non-archived characters) is browseable in the studio; selecting one shows detail without leaving center.
  • Selected character shows a Role in the story summary composed from existing notes + character-sheet desire/pressure/voice (deterministic; no provider call).
  • Relationships render as a visual constellation (center ego + linked peers with kind-labeled edges), with click-to-open peer and existing link add/remove still available.
  • Linked scenes render as a presence strip/list with jump to Draft; empty states stay clear.
  • Narrow layout remains usable (roster + detail stack; no blank center).
  • Focused UI tests + typecheck; pnpm verify before handoff; Playwright deferred.

  4. CP4 — Docs + handoff: PRODUCT/ARCHITECTURE shell notes; revise cursor-shell decision that preferred Characters in primary; record log + WHERE-I-LEFT-OFF.

  • D3 (proposed): Role summary is writer-authored field composition (notes + desire/pressure/voice), not BYOK generation in this plan.
