Ghostwriter delivery plan · Structure / cast

Character screens depth

Make Cast a first-class center workspace with a readable character dossier, deterministic role summary, relationship constellation, and scene presence — far deeper than today’s cramped primary-side browse panel — without inventing a second story store or confusing knowledge links with Canvas Thread Trace.

Intent

Writers need to see who a character is in the story: role, desire/pressure/voice, aliases, bonds, and where they appear — without losing the manuscript tree. Today Characters rail replaces Explorer with a flat CharacterBrowsePanel. This plan moves Cast into the center (same hosting class as Title Page / Plans), keeps Explorer as the only wide primary content, and deepens the dossier with a composed Role summary, static ego constellation for knowledge links, and a scene presence strip that jumps to Draft on the same scene ID.

Explicitly out of scope: force-directed / physics graph libraries; AI-authored role summaries (Role remains deterministic composition); new canonical relationship kinds; Canvas board rewrite or Thread Trace changes; evidence-chip passage domain (UI may show proposed chips in Split Sheet only; persistence waits a later ADR); Character Coach conversation redesign (stays Mockups 4.0 / harness); Playwright until founder verifies the complete outcome. Character visual gallery (generate / upload / delete) was added by founder revision 2026-07-25.

Product thesis and authority

  • Center for people, tree for structure: Characters rail opens Cast in the center; Explorer stays primary on wide.
  • One knowledge ID: Tree, Cast, Split Sheet, Canvas story-record card, and Draft cast share StoryKnowledgeId.
  • Dossier over form soup: Identity → Role → sheet → notes → constellation → scenes, not a cramped sidebar scroll of equal-weight fields.
  • Two relationship UIs stay distinct: Cast constellation edits story-knowledge links (K↔K). Map Relationships lens projects canvas links. Never one store pretending to be both.
  • Role is composed: Deterministic projection from notes + desire/pressure/voice. Opening Cast never calls a provider.
  • Craft ≠ prose: Sheet and knowledge mutations use existing project metadata / storyKnowledge commands; scene working revision stays untouched.
  • Write handoff: Scene presence and “Open in Split Sheet” reuse Mockups 4.0 Write chrome; Coach remains propose → apply.

Acceptance criteria

  1. On wide, Characters rail opens Cast in the center; primary side remains Explorer (tree visible). Plans / Title Page center ownership closes or yields cleanly when Cast opens and vice versa.
  2. Cast studio shows a browseable roster of all non-archived characters with scene count, link count, and authority; selecting one shows the dossier without leaving center.
  3. Selected character dossier shows: label, aliases, authority, Notes, Character sheet (desire / pressure / voice), and a Role in the story summary composed deterministically from notes + sheet (no provider call).
  4. Relationships render as a static ego constellation (center + peers with kind-labeled edges) with click-to-open peer and existing add/remove knowledge-link commands. Narrow may use a kind-grouped list fallback. Empty relationship state is explicit.
  5. UI copy and empty states make clear that Cast links are knowledge links — not Canvas Thread Trace.
  6. Linked scenes render as a presence strip/list with POV marker when the scene’s povStoryKnowledgeId matches; Open Draft jumps to that scene ID; return to Cast preserves selection when practical.
  7. Create character, archive/restore (visible archived treatment), and edit notes/sheet/aliases remain available from the studio with truthful save/conflict behavior (command applied nothing on conflict).
  8. Narrow layout stacks roster → detail with no blank center; constellation may collapse to list; scene rows remain tappable.
  9. Split · Sheet (Write) retains scene-scoped cast link/unlink and gains an “Open in Cast studio” affordance that lands on the same character in center Cast (Mockups 4.0 alignment).
  10. MCP navigator read model includes characterSheet fields already present on web navigator (parity fix) or documents an explicit exception — no silent omission after this plan.
  11. Focused Vitest for host helpers, role-summary / constellation projections, roster selection; typecheck; pnpm verify before handoff. Playwright deferred until founder verifies.
  12. Keyboard and screen-reader paths exist for roster, dossier fields, peer open, and scene jump; reduced-motion does not rely on constellation animation.

Domain and design audit

Canonical objects (authoritative: ADR 0008 / 0009 + domain.ts)

  • StoryKnowledge (kind: character): StoryKnowledgeId, label, authority, notes, aliases, characterSheet { desire, pressure, voiceNotes }, archivedAt.
  • Knowledge links: directed linkedKnowledge[] with kinds cast | theme | development-cycle | breadcrumb | related.
  • Scene presence: linkedSceneIds + optional Scene.povStoryKnowledgeId.
  • Not in scope as new tables: portraits, evidence passage links, relationship strength scores, per-book character forks (ADR 0003 shared series characters).

Concurrency / persistence

  • Mutations via existing storyKnowledge.* project commands and project.version metadata domain — no new optimistic domain.
  • Role summary and constellation models are pure projections (client or shared helpers); not stored rows.
